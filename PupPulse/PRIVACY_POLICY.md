# PupPulse Privacy Policy

**Effective Date: September 7, 2025**

## Information We Collect

### Personal Information
- Dog profile information (name, breed, age, weight)
- Health event data (walks, meals, symptoms, medications)
- Contact information for emergency purposes

### Health Data
- Pulse score calculations
- Symptom tracking
- Medication schedules
- Veterinary visit records

### Usage Data
- App interaction patterns
- Feature usage statistics
- Performance metrics

## How We Use Your Information

- Provide AI-powered health insights
- Generate personalized care recommendations
- Send health reminders and notifications
- Improve app functionality and user experience
- Emergency contact and veterinary coordination

## Data Security

- All data is encrypted in transit and at rest
- We use industry-standard security measures
- Your data is never sold to third parties
- You can delete your data at any time

## Third-Party Services

- We use secure cloud services for data storage
- AI analysis is performed using encrypted APIs
- Emergency services integration for urgent situations

## Your Rights

- Access your data
- Correct inaccurate information
- Delete your account and data
- Opt-out of non-essential communications

## Contact Us

For privacy questions: privacy@puppulse.app

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. We'll notify you of significant changes.
